[03] Controls

How it plays

Tap

Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer.

Hold

Hold to drift. Sliding is the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins.

Brake

Press the brake button in the middle to slow down. Brake mid-drift and the turn tightens.

[04] Features

The game

The Chase is Everything

Every race is a single, high-stakes run down a mountain road. It’s just you and the rival ahead: no laps, no crowded grid. Catch them before the finish line, and the road is yours. A race takes about a minute, so it’s a car racing game that fits into any short break.

Drift to Survive

Drifting isn’t just for style. It’s the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins. Nail the drift angle and you’ll exit the corner faster than gripping around it. Touch a wall, though, and the drift points you were building are gone.

Five Crews, One Mountain

Take on 30 rivals from five street crews across 16 mountain passes. Each crew is a chapter of the career: race its drivers, pass a drift challenge, then face the crew’s King. Finish a race and the next stage opens, even if you lose, so one tough rival never stops your run. Beat all five Kings to reach the finale.

Endless Drift

A mountain road that never ends. Every wall you hit damages your car, and every rival you overtake repairs it. See how far you can go before the car gives out. Unlocks once you beat your first King.

Solo Drift

Want the road to yourself? Run any pass in Solo Drift and go for all three stars with your best drift score.

24 Cars, Earned by Driving

Every race earns stars, even the ones you lose, and stars unlock new cars. Beat a King and their car can join your garage. There’s no tuning or upgrade grind: pick a car and drive.

Play Offline

There are no logins and no internet connection needed to race. Your save lives on your device, so you can play this racing game completely offline.

No Paywalls

Drift Versus is free to play, and everything can be earned just by driving. There’s an optional upgrade that removes forced ads, plus currency packs, but nothing is locked behind a paywall.

[05] Questions

Quick answers

Is Drift Versus free?

Yes. Drift Versus is free to play and every car can be earned by driving. It shows ads between races. An optional one-time upgrade removes those forced ads; rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus, stay. There are also optional currency packs.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Racing needs no internet connection, no account and no login.

Is there multiplayer?

No. Drift Versus is single player: every race is a one-on-one duel against an AI rival, so there is no lobby and no waiting.

How do the controls work?

Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer and hold to drift. A brake button in the middle slows you down, and braking mid-drift tightens the turn. The car accelerates on its own.

How long is a race?

About a minute. Each race is a single run down a mountain pass with no laps.

What if I keep losing to a rival?

Finishing a race opens the next stage even if you lose, and losses still earn stars. A crew’s King opens once you have cleared four of the six stages before them.

Can I tune or upgrade my car?

No. There is no upgrade grind: you unlock a car and drive it as it is.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is saved only on your device, and the game has no accounts, so it cannot be transferred. Remove Ads can be restored on any device signed in to the same store account. More on the support page.

Is Drift Versus on iPhone?

Not yet. It is out on Android now and coming soon to the App Store.
