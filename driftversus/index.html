How it plays

Tap

The car drives itself. Tap the left or right side of the screen to swerve.

Hold

Hold a side and the car drifts. Switch sides mid-slide to swing it the other way.

Brake

Press the brake button in the middle to slow down. Brake mid-drift and the turn tightens.

The game

Chase, don't lap

Every race is a single run down a one-lane mountain pass with a rival ahead. No laps, no position table. Just the gap, closing or opening.

Drift to go faster

On a one-lane road, sliding is how you carry speed through a corner. Link drifts to build your score. Clip the wall and that drift's points are gone.

About a minute a race

Made for one hand and short breaks. Start a race at the bus stop and finish it before the bus comes.

Beat the six Legends

A 48-stage career across 16 mountain passes in four districts. Race 24 rivals from four crews on the way to six Legends. Beat a Legend and their car is yours to unlock.

Three stars a stage

One star for the win, two more for a high score, and even a lost race can earn those two. Fill the star bar to earn car tokens for any of 23 cars.

Solo Drift

No rival, just the road. Chase a score on any pass you have raced and earn up to three road stars.

Free to play with ads. Car token packs and an optional purchase to remove the between-race ads are available, and every car can be earned by driving. Your garage is saved on your device.
