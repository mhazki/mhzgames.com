[03] Controls

How it plays

Tap

Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer.

Hold

Hold to drift. Sliding is the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins.

Brake

Press the brake button in the middle to slow down. Brake mid-drift and the turn tightens.

[04] Features

The game

The Chase is Everything

Every race is a single, high-stakes run down a mountain road. It’s just you and the rival ahead: no laps, no crowded grid. Catch them before the finish line, and the road is yours. A race takes about a minute, so it’s a car racing game that fits into any short break.

Drift to Survive

Drifting isn’t just for style. It’s the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins. Nail the drift angle and you’ll exit the corner faster than gripping around it. Touch a wall, though, and the drift points you were building are gone.

Rise Through the Legends

Take on 24 rivals from four crews across 16 mountain passes. Beat each Legend of the mountain, earn stars and build your garage with 23 unique cars, every one of them unlockable just by driving.

Solo Drift

Want the road to yourself? Run any pass in Solo Drift and go for all three stars with your best drift score.

Play Offline

There are no logins and no internet connection needed to race. Your save lives on your device, so you can play this racing game completely offline.

No Paywalls

Drift Versus is free to play, and everything can be earned just by driving. There’s an optional upgrade that removes forced ads, plus currency packs, but nothing is locked behind a paywall.
