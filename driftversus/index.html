How it plays

Tap

Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer.

Hold

Hold to drift. Sliding is the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins.

Brake

Press the brake button in the middle to slow down. Brake mid-drift and the turn tightens.

The game

The Chase is Everything

Every race is a high-stakes, single run down a narrow mountain pass. It’s just you and the rival ahead. Catch them before the finish line, and the road is yours.

Drift to Survive

Sliding isn’t just a trick you do for style, drifting is the only way to carry your speed through sharp hairpins. Nail the angle and you’ll rocket out of the corner faster than you entered.

Rise Through the Legends

Take on the legends of the mountain. Work your way up, defeat each legend, and build up your garage with 23 unique cars, all of which can be unlocked entirely through gameplay.

Play Offline

There are no logins and no mandatory internet connection. Your save lives on your device, so you can play completely offline.

No Paywalls

The game is free to play, everything can be earned just by driving. While there is an optional ad-free upgrade and currency packs available, absolutely nothing is locked behind a paywall.
