[03] Controls

How it plays

Tap

Tap the left or right half of the screen to shift one lane. That is the entire control scheme.

Draft

Catch the rival ahead and tuck into its slipstream, then slingshot out and pass.

Shift up

Every pass shifts you up a gear for the rest of the run: more speed, more traffic to thread.

[04] Features

The game

You Set the Difficulty

There is always one rival ahead, running a little faster than the traffic. Hang back and cruise at the gear you are on, or chase every rival and see how fast the road can get before it takes you. Nothing forces you forward.

One Way to Crash

Rear-end a car and the run is over. Clip one on the side and it costs you a gear, not the run. Distance is the score, and your best is the only number the game keeps.

THE LOT

A second mode: a car parking jam puzzle. Swipe each car out of a packed lot in the right order, time your exits around the police patrol, and clear the lot. A failed lot restarts in one tap, never an ad.

Fill Your Garage

Clearing lots earns Stacks, and Stacks buy new cars for your garage.

Free to Play

Highway Versus is free. One optional purchase removes the ad that appears between some runs, and there are optional Stack packs. Watching an ad to revive is always your choice.
